The road to the platinum

Six stages, in the order a clean run should take them. The order is built from the official descriptions: the story first, the missable stranger chains before the endgame closes their windows, the counters and collectibles as parallel grinds, and the money and 100% checklist last. Your progress in each stage fills in as you tick trophies off.

Leonida Legend is an unofficial, offline fan guide. Trophy names, descriptions and grades are as PlayStation publishes them; every guide is written for this app and cites its sources beside the trophy. Progress is stored only in your browser (localStorage) — export it to keep a backup. Not affiliated with Rockstar Games or PlayStation.
